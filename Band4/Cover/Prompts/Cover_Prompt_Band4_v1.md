# Cover-Prompt – Band 4: Die zugemauerte Tür (Vorderseite, produktionsfertig)

## Design-Philosophie Band 4

| Regel | Umsetzung |
|-------|-----------|
| **70% Hintergrund** | Das kalte Zimmer + die freigelegte, einen Spalt offene alte Tür — eisiges Blaugrau/Frost, Raureif, verlassener Altbau |
| **20% Überschrift** | „DIE ZUGEMAUERTE TÜR" — schwere gravierte Versalien, kalkweiß mit warmem Lichtsaum, groß aber ausbalanciert (bleibt in oberen ~28–30%, überlappt die Tür NICHT), „TÜR" auf Breite der Zeile darüber |
| **10% Hingucker** | Das WARME GOLD-BERNSTEIN-Licht aus dem Türspalt (eine stillstehende Standuhr, Fabers Raum) + Schattens bernsteinfarbene Augen — die einzigen warmen Punkte |
| **Differenzierung B1/B2/B3** | B1 = Altbau + Indigo/Lila · B2 = Friedhof + Waldgrün · B3 = U-Bahn + Stahlblau → **B4 = kaltes Zimmer + warmes Gold aus der Tür** |
| **Serien-Markenmoment** | Schatten drängt zum zweiten Mal HINEIN — diesmal in etwas Warmes, Einladendes. Gerade das Warme macht es unheimlich („warum will er da rein?") |
| **Vertikale Staffelung** | Titel oben (30%) → glühende Tür Mitte (Hingucker, 40–58%) → Kinder+Schatten als Silhouetten unten (62–100%). Titel & Tür überlappen NIE. |
| **Thumbnail-Anker** | Schatten ist die größte Vordergrund-Silhouette; seine zwei Bernstein-Augen + der goldene Türspalt tragen die Fernwirkung (auch bei 100px lesbar). |
| **Ton** | „Kribbeln, kein Albtraum" — der Grusel wispert (eine stehende Uhr, Nebel, eine kaum sichtbare offene Hand), er brüllt nicht |
| **Typografie** | Teil der Komposition, nicht Overlay. Alle Texte im Bild gerendert. Titel-Lesbarkeit hat Vorrang vor dem Untertitel. |

---

## Cover-Texte (ALLE im Bild zu rendern — exakt so)

- **Reihen-Zeile (klein, oben):** `DIE GEISTERSPÜRER · BAND 4`
- **Haupttitel (groß, dominant):** `DIE ZUGEMAUERTE TÜR`
- **Untertitel (klein, unter Titel):** `Ein Grusel-Abenteuer für Kinder ab 10 Jahren`
- **Autor (unten):** `Benjamin Krug`

_(Kein Badge/Siegel — bewusst weggelassen, weil KI-Generatoren kleinen Rund-Text unlesbar rendern. Das USP „Kribbeln statt Albträume" steht in KDP-Beschreibung + Keywords. Falls doch gewünscht: nachträglich in Canva als eigenes Element sauber setzen.)_

## Verwendung

### Variante A: ChatGPT (GPT-4o mit Bildgenerierung) — empfohlen für Text

1. ChatGPT öffnen (GPT-4o / DALL·E).
2. Den Prompt unten (Codeblock) komplett einfügen.
3. Als **ersten Satz** davorstellen:
   *„Generate a children's book cover illustration based on the following specification. Render ALL German typographic elements exactly as written — they are part of the illustration, not an overlay. Spell every German word correctly, including the umlauts Ü and Ä."*
4. Nach der Generation iterativ verfeinern (Checkliste am Ende).

### Variante B: Midjourney (v6.1 / v7)

Kompakter Midjourney-Prompt im Appendix. ACHTUNG: Text-Rendering (v.a. deutsche Umlaute) unzuverlässig → Typografie in Canva/Affinity nachsetzen. Für die deutschen Texte ist ChatGPT/DALL·E oder nachträgliche Beschriftung sicherer.

## HAUPT-PROMPT (Vorderseite)

```
Children's book cover illustration in a modern, semi-realistic digital painting style with slightly stylized cartoon proportions. Painterly visible brushstrokes, rich textures, cinematic lighting. It should feel like a movie poster for a middle-grade ghost-adventure film for ages 10–12. NOT chibi, NOT manga, NOT flat-color cartoon, NOT photorealistic, NOT cute or babyish.

VERTICAL BOOK COVER, 1600 x 2560 px (KDP 6x9 ratio). Built around ONE central image: an old wooden door, walled up long ago, now uncovered and standing a hand's width open — with warm golden light spilling out of the gap into a freezing cold room.

CRITICAL VERTICAL LAYOUT (read top to bottom, like a movie poster — this ordering prevents the title and the glowing door from fighting for the same space):
- TOP ~30% : a calm, dark, frosted upper wall/ceiling corner. The TITLE TYPOGRAPHY sits here, clearly readable against uniform dark tones.
- UPPER-MIDDLE ~40–58% : the walled-up DOOR, open a crack, its warm golden glow — the visual HINGUCKER, the brightest thing on the cover, centered.
- LOWER THIRD ~62–100% : the two children and the dog, seen from behind as SILHOUETTES rimmed in the golden door-light, standing on the cold floor and facing the glow.
Leave a small buffer of dark wall between the bottom of the title and the top of the door so the two never overlap.

════════════════════════════════════════
FOREGROUND (lower third, ~62–100%) — THE DOG (dominant) + THE TWO CHILDREN
════════════════════════════════════════

In the cold room's lower third, seen from behind as dark SILHOUETTES rimmed by the golden door-light. Their breath fogs faintly in the cold air. They face the glowing door above/ahead of them. Keep the figures readable as clean simple silhouettes so they still work at thumbnail size — the DOG is the largest and most central foreground shape.

SCHATTEN (the dog) — THE MAIN FOREGROUND FIGURE, slightly larger and more central than in earlier books:
- Medium-sized mixed-breed dog with dark, almost-black fur, seen from behind/side, low to the ground.
- LUMINOUS AMBER EYES (#d4920b) — turned just enough that BOTH glowing eyes are visible in near-profile; they are the warmest, most alive points in the whole image and glow from within. These two amber points are the cover's thumbnail anchor: they must stay clearly visible and bright even when the cover is tiny.
- CRITICAL POSTURE — THIS IS THE STORY OF THE COVER: Schatten does NOT refuse. He LEANS FORWARD and low toward the glowing door, ears forward, nose toward the light, body eager, the leash taut, gently pulling Nora IN. For the second time in the series he wants to go INTO the danger — and this time the danger looks warm and inviting, which makes it far more unsettling.

NORA (girl, 12) — LEFT, just behind and above the dog, smaller in frame:
- Shoulder-length straight brown hair, seen from behind/three-quarter back, NOT her full face.
- Dark TEAL zip-up hoodie (#2a8a7a) — the most saturated COOL color on a figure (series-consistent). Even as a near-silhouette, a hint of teal catches the light on her shoulder.
- Holding a small flashlight low; its cold beam is almost washed out by the warm door-light.
- Posture: wary, leaning slightly back, one hand held back — she does NOT trust the warm light, while the dog pulls her forward.

THEO (boy, 10) — RIGHT, half a step BEHIND Nora, smallest figure:
- Messy, slightly curly dark-blond hair, seen from behind.
- Oversized OLIVE-GREEN BOMBER JACKET (military olive green — NOT brown), a hint of olive catching the light.
- One hand gripping his backpack strap; body angled as if he wants to retreat.

════════════════════════════════════════
MIDDLE GROUND (center) — THE WALLED-UP DOOR, NOW OPEN A CRACK
════════════════════════════════════════

The whole back wall of the cold room. Most of it is rough, hastily applied, over-painted PLASTER — pale, uneven, finger-smeared, with a faint bloom of FROST and ice crystals across it (this room is unnaturally cold). A heavy old wooden wardrobe has been pushed aside to the edge, revealing what the plaster was hiding:

An ancient DARK WOODEN DOOR, its frame just chipped free of the old plaster, with a black wrought-iron handle, corroded with age. The door stands OPEN a hand's width — a vertical slit of WARM GOLDEN LIGHT pouring out into the cold blue room. Around the door's edges, thin curls of pale cold mist meet the warm light.

The contrast is the point: the ROOM is cold, blue, frosted, empty. What is behind the door is WARM, golden, and full.

════════════════════════════════════════
BEHIND THE DOOR (the warm impossible room) — CLOCKS, MIST, A HINT OF A HAND
════════════════════════════════════════

Through the narrow glowing gap, only a sliver is visible — but it is clearly a room far too deep to fit behind a wall. Directly in the gap, ONE tall old GRANDFATHER CLOCK stands in clear silhouette, its round brass face catching the warm gold light and its pendulum FROZEN mid-swing, hanging at an impossible angle (time has stopped in there). Behind and beyond it, softly blurred in the golden haze, the faint suggestion of MORE clocks and shelves receding impossibly far — kept as a soft impression, NOT many sharp detailed objects. Warm dust drifts in the golden shaft of light. One clear clock in front, endless warm depth suggested behind it.

THE HINGUCKER — THE WARM GOLD LIGHT:
- The golden-amber glow from the door gap (#e0a63c warm core, #c8862a deeper) is the primary color accent — warm, inviting, and WRONG in this frozen room.
- Faint brass reflections on the nearest clock faces.

GHOST HINT (EXTREMELY subtle — NO clear figure, NO face):
- Deep inside the warm light, half-lost among the clock silhouettes, the barest suggestion of a single OPEN, RESTING GREY HAND — palm turned upward and relaxed, half-hidden behind a clock, as if quietly waiting rather than calling. It is gentle and a little sad, NOT reaching, NOT grabbing, NOT clawing, fingers NOT curled. So faint it reads as "wait… is someone in there?" — NOT "a ghost is coming for us." Keep it ambiguous and calm. Do NOT render a face, an arm, or a whole figure — only the faint hint of one open hand.

════════════════════════════════════════

════════════════════════════════════════

The title MUST read exactly "DIE ZUGEMAUERTE TÜR" — the last word is T-Ü-R with an Ü, never "TUR" or "TUER". The series line MUST read "DIE GEISTERSPÜRER" with an Ü. The subtitle MUST read "für" with ü. Double-check the spelling of every letter before finishing. If unsure about the umlaut, render the two dots above the U clearly.

TOP ZONE (upper ~30%): the cold upper wall / dark ceiling corner of the room — relatively uniform dark, frosted blue-grey tones so the title is readable. ALL title text stays within this top ~30% band; keep a dark buffer between the title's bottom edge and the top of the glowing door below.

SERIES LINE (small, at the very top):
"DIE GEISTERSPÜRER · BAND 4"
— thin, widely spaced capitals, cold steel-grey (#9aa6b0). Correct umlaut: Ü.

MAIN TITLE (large and clearly the dominant text, but well-balanced — do NOT let it overpower the whole cover):
"DIE ZUGEMAUERTE TÜR"
- Heavy, engraved/carved capitals, slightly condensed, chalk-bone-white (#e8e6e0) with a hard dark shadow for depth.
- Large and confident, filling most of the cover width, BUT the entire title block stays within the top ~28–30% of the image — it must NOT extend down into or over the glowing door. Leave clear dark breathing space between the bottom of the title and the door below.
- Stacked on 3 lines, centered: "DIE ZUGEMAUERTE" as the two upper lines, "TÜR" as the bottom line. IMPORTANT: scale "TÜR" so it is about as WIDE as the line above it — do NOT let "TÜR" shrink into a small third line, but also do NOT let it grow so tall that it crowds the door. All three lines should read as one balanced title block of similar weight.
- The lower edges of the letters catch a faint WARM GOLDEN rim-light from the door below, tying the title to the glowing door.
- One subtle detail: a hairline crack or a seam of frost runs through one letter, echoing the plaster and ice.
- Correct German spelling and umlaut: "TÜR" with Ü.

SUBTITLE (small, directly below the title, with a comfortable gap):
"Ein Grusel-Abenteuer für Kinder ab 10 Jahren"
— small, clean sans-serif, steel-grey, on a single line. Correct umlaut: für.
- Leave a clear gap of dark wall between the subtitle and the top of the glowing door — the subtitle must NOT touch or sit directly on the door. It stays up in the title zone.
- PRIORITY: the MAIN TITLE stays the dominant text; the subtitle is small and unobtrusive.

AUTHOR (bottom edge, centered):
"Benjamin Krug"
— small centered capitals or title-case, light grey.

Keep the LOWER-RIGHT corner of the cover visually calm and free of important detail or text (a print barcode is added there later — do not place artwork focal points or lettering in the bottom-right ~5x3 cm).

NO badge, seal, sticker, ribbon or emblem anywhere on the cover — keep the corners clean.

════════════════════════════════════════
LIGHTING
════════════════════════════════════════

1. PRIMARY — HINGUCKER: The WARM GOLDEN LIGHT from the door gap. It is the strongest light source, pooling warm gold onto the frosted wall, the floor, the children's edges and the dog. This warm-in-a-cold-room glow is the emotional core.
2. SECONDARY: The children's cold flashlight beam — thin, bluish, almost overpowered by the golden light (showing how weak/ordinary they are against what's behind the door).
3. ACCENT: Schatten's amber eyes (#d4920b) — tiny warm points that rhyme with the door light.
4. Cold pale-blue frost-light on the plaster wall and in the children's fogging breath.

Overall: A freezing, quiet, ordinary room — and one impossible warm door that should not exist. NOT pitch black; every foreground element clearly readable. The warmth behind the door is beautiful and deeply wrong.
```

## Checkliste nach der Generation (iterativ verfeinern)

- [ ] **Titel korrekt geschrieben:** „DIE ZUGEMAUERTE TÜR" (mit Ü!), „DIE GEISTERSPÜRER · BAND 4" (mit Ü), „für Kinder ab 10 Jahren" (mit ü).
- [ ] **Alle vier Textblöcke vorhanden** (Reihe, Titel, Untertitel, Autor) und lesbar.
- [ ] **70/20/10 gewahrt:** kaltes Zimmer dominiert, Titel klar, warmes Gold ist der einzige starke Farbakzent.
- [ ] **Schatten zieht HINEIN** (nicht weg), bernsteinfarbene Augen leuchten.
- [ ] **Nora teal Hoodie**, **Theo olivgrüne Bomberjacke** — Farben stimmen (Serien-Konsistenz).
- [ ] **Kinder von hinten/halbes Profil** — keine vollen Gesichter (Serien-Regel).
- [ ] **Warm-vs-kalt-Kontrast** deutlich (frostiges Zimmer, goldener Türspalt).
- [ ] **Geist-Hint ambig** — die offene ruhende Hand ist eine Andeutung, kein klares Wesen, kein Gesicht, kein Greifen.
- [ ] **Vertikale Staffelung stimmt:** Titel oben, Tür Mitte, Figuren unten — Titel und Tür überlappen NICHT.
- [ ] **★ THUMBNAIL-TEST:** Cover auf ~100px Höhe verkleinern. Erkennt man dann noch: goldener Türspalt + zwei leuchtende Augen + der Titel? Wenn ja → gut. Wenn die Figuren zu Matsch werden → Schatten größer/klarer machen.
- [ ] **Eine klare Standuhr** im Türspalt (Pendel stillstehend), nicht Uhren-Matsch.
- [ ] **★ Titel groß, aber ausbalanciert** — „TÜR" auf Breite der Zeile darüber, ABER der Titel bleibt oben (~28–30%) und überlappt die Tür NICHT. Klarer dunkler Abstand zwischen Titel/Untertitel und Tür.
- [ ] **Untertitel touchiert die Tür nicht** — bleibt oben in der Titelzone, mit Luft zur Tür.
- [ ] **Kein Badge/Siegel** — Ecken sauber (Badge bewusst weggelassen).
- [ ] **Kein Barcode/Text unten rechts** — dort bleibt Platz frei (KDP druckt Barcode selbst).
- [ ] Palette hebt sich klar von B1 (Lila), B2 (Waldgrün), B3 (Stahlblau) ab.

> **⚠️ Titel-Balance ist Glückssache beim Generator.** Wenn der Titel zu groß gerät (drängt über die Tür) oder „TÜR" zu klein/zu groß wird: mehrmals neu würfeln, oder den Titel in **Canva/Affinity** sauber setzen (Bild ohne Titel generieren = obere Zone frei, dann Titel als Text drüberlegen — perfekte Größe, Balance, Umlaut). **Badge:** bewusst weggelassen (KI rendert kleinen Rund-Text unlesbar). Falls doch gewünscht → in Canva als eigenes Element setzen, nie per KI. Das aktuelle Bild ist bereits sehr gut — nur die Titel-Balance ist der letzte Feinschliff.
