# Cover-Prompts – Band 3: Schatten sieht mehr (3 Varianten)

## Design-Philosophie Band 3

| Regel | Umsetzung |
|-------|-----------|
| **70% Hintergrund** | Stillgelegte U-Bahn-Station / Tunnelmund — kalte Stahlblau-Schwarz-Palette, Staub, alte Kacheln |
| **20% Überschrift** | "SCHATTEN SIEHT MEHR" — industrielle Gravur-Optik, kalkweiß, ~40% Bildhöhe |
| **10% Hingucker** | Der grüne Schein des alten Bahnsignals + Schattens bernsteinfarbene Augen — die einzigen warmen/farbigen Punkte |
| **Differenzierung B1/B2** | B1 = Altbau + Indigoblau-Lila → B2 = Friedhof + Waldgrün-Schwarz → **B3 = U-Bahn-Tunnel + Stahlblau-Schwarz** |
| **Serien-Markenmoment** | Schatten zieht zum ersten Mal HINEIN statt zu verweigern (verkehrtes Verhalten) |
| **Typografie** | Teil der Komposition — nicht nachträglich. Breite Textzone in der oberen Bildmitte |

## Verwendung

## VARIANTE 1 – "Der Tunnelmund" (EMPFOHLEN)
### Kernidee: Spannung durch verkehrtes Verhalten

In Band 1 und 2 hat Schatten gewarnt und verweigert. Hier zieht er zum ersten Mal HINEIN — in die Schwärze. Das ist das Bild, das einen Leser der Reihe sofort fesselt: Wenn der Geisterhund freiwillig in den dunklen Tunnel will, ist das das schlechteste Zeichen aller Zeiten.

```
Children's book cover illustration in a modern, semi-realistic digital painting style with slight cartoon proportions. Painterly visible brushstrokes, rich textures, and cinematic lighting. The style should feel like a movie poster for a middle-grade horror-adventure film for ages 10-12. NOT chibi, NOT manga, NOT flat-color cartoon, NOT photorealistic, NOT cute or childish.

VERTICAL BOOK COVER, 1600x2560 px (KDP 6x9 ratio). Composition split into three depth layers.

════════════════════════════════════════
FOREGROUND (bottom 45%) — THE TWO CHILDREN AND THE DOG
════════════════════════════════════════

Three figures stand at the top of a wide staircase that descends into darkness — the entrance to an abandoned underground subway station. They are seen from slightly behind and to the side; their backs and partial profiles are visible, NOT their full faces. They look DOWN into the black stairwell.

LEFT — NORA (girl, 12 years old):
- Slightly taller of the two children
- Shoulder-length straight brown hair
- Wearing a dark TEAL zip-up hoodie (#2a8a7a) — her hoodie is the MOST SATURATED non-accent color in the foreground (consistent with Band 1 & 2)
- Dark jeans, worn sneakers
- Holding a small flashlight; its narrow beam points down the stairs into the dark
- Posture: tense, leaning slightly back — she does NOT want to go down, but the dog is pulling her

RIGHT — THEO (boy, 10 years old):
- Shorter than Nora by about a head
- Messy, slightly curly dark-blond hair
- Wearing an oversized OLIVE-GREEN BOMBER JACKET (military olive green — NOT brown)
- Dark cargo pants, worn-out sneakers
- One hand gripping his backpack strap, the other half-raised — he wants to run
- He stands half a step BEHIND Nora

CENTER-FRONT (between/ahead of the children) — SCHATTEN (the dog):
- Medium-sized mixed-breed dog with dark, almost black fur
- LUMINOUS AMBER-COLORED EYES (#d4920b) — one of only two warm color sources in the image. They glow faintly from within.
- CRITICAL POSTURE — THIS IS THE STORY OF THE COVER: Schatten is NOT refusing. He LEANS FORWARD and DOWN toward the dark staircase, the leash taut, pulling Nora. His body points INTO the darkness. Ears forward, nose down, eager. For the first time he wants to go IN. This reversal is the emotional hook of the image.

════════════════════════════════════════
MIDDLE GROUND (center) — THE STAIRWELL & RUSTED GATE
════════════════════════════════════════

A wide STAIRCASE descends from the children's feet into pitch blackness. The first three or four steps are lit by the flashlight (old stone steps, cracked, dust-covered); below that, the stairs vanish into total black.

To one side, a half-open RUSTED IRON GATE — wrought iron bars, heavily corroded, partly pushed aside. Above it, carved into weathered stone, a barely legible sign: "ALTSTADT-NORD".

On the tiled wall beside the stairs: old cracked subway tiles (off-white, grimy), and a faded 1972 advertising poster, peeling, almost illegible.

════════════════════════════════════════
DEEP BACKGROUND (the darkness below) — THE STATION & THE SIGNAL
════════════════════════════════════════

Far down in the black, barely visible, the suggestion of an old subway platform: a hint of a curved tiled wall, the dark mouth of a tunnel.

THE HINGUCKER — THE OLD RAILWAY SIGNAL:
- Deep in the dark, hanging from a rusted arm, a small round antique signal bell glows with a faint, COLD GREENISH-WHITE light (#7fae9a) — as if it is ringing on its own in a station with no power. This is the second, larger color accent — eerie, not warm.
- A thin, cold pale-blue mist drifts up from the very bottom of the stairwell.

GHOST HINT (extremely subtle — NO clear figure):
- In the deepest darkness, where the mist is faintly lighter, the barest suggestion of an outstretched hand or a pale vertical shape — so faint it reads as "wait... is something down there?" NOT "a ghost is standing there." Do NOT render a clear person. Keep it ambiguous.

════════════════════════════════════════
TITLE TYPOGRAPHY (integrated into the upper image)
════════════════════════════════════════

TOP ZONE (upper 35%): dark tunnel ceiling / tiled arch — relatively uniform dark tones so the title is readable.

SERIES LINE (small, top): "DIE GEISTERSPÜRER · BAND 3" — thin spaced capitals, cold steel-gray (#9aa6b0).

MAIN TITLE (large, dominant): "SCHATTEN SIEHT MEHR"
- Bold industrial/engraved capitals, slightly condensed, chalk-bone-white (#e8e6e0) with a hard dark shadow for depth
- Stacked on 2–3 lines, centered, occupying ~40% of image height in the upper-middle zone
- A faint cold-blue glow rims the letters, as if lit by the signal far below
- One subtle detail: a hairline crack runs through one letter, echoing the cracked tiles

SUBTITLE (small, below title): "Ein Grusel-Abenteuer für Kinder ab 10 Jahren" — small clean sans-serif, steel-gray.

AUTHOR (bottom edge): "Benjamin Krug" — small, centered, light gray.

════════════════════════════════════════
LIGHTING
════════════════════════════════════════

1. PRIMARY: The children's flashlight — a cold narrow beam pointing down the stairs, catching dust particles in the air and the first steps.
2. SECONDARY — HINGUCKER 1: The green-white glow of the railway signal far below (#7fae9a) — eerie cold light pooling in the deep dark.
3. ACCENT — HINGUCKER 2: Schatten's amber eyes (#d4920b) — tiny warm points, the only truly warm light in the image.
4. Cold pale-blue mist rising from the bottom adds faint internal luminescence.

Overall: Dark, cold, underground, claustrophobic. NOT pitch black — every foreground element clearly readable. The depth of the stairwell mysterious.

════════════════════════════════════════
COLOR PALETTE — 70/20/10 DISTRIBUTION
════════════════════════════════════════

DOMINANT (70% — underground atmosphere):
- Near-black steel blue (#0c1218)
- Dark charcoal gray (#1a1c1f)
- Cold tile off-white-gray (#b8bcc0)
- Rust-brown iron (#3d2b1f)
- Cold pale-blue mist (#5a6a78)

TYPOGRAPHIC (20% — title zone):
- Chalk-bone-white (#e8e6e0) — title letters
- Deep shadow-black (#0a0a0a) — letter depth/shadow
- Steel-gray (#9aa6b0) — series line / subtitle

ACCENT (10% — Hingucker):
- Railway-signal green-white glow (#7fae9a)
- Schatten amber eyes (#d4920b)

NO purple/indigo (that is Band 1). NO forest green (that is Band 2). The cold STEEL-BLUE + the eerie signal-green clearly mark this as Band 3.

════════════════════════════════════════
MOOD
════════════════════════════════════════

A reader who knows the series sees the dog pulling INTO the dark and feels their stomach drop. A new reader sees a staircase into blackness, a faint green glow, two amber eyes, and wants to know what is down there. "Kribbeln, kein Albtraum" — eerie and inviting, never gory.
```

## VARIANTE 2 – "Der Bahnsteig" (Alternative)
### Kernidee: Der Ort selbst — die schlafende Station

Wenn Variante 1 zu eng/dunkel wirkt, zeigt diese Variante mehr vom Schauplatz: den langen verlassenen Bahnsteig, das läutende Signal, die Kinder klein davor.

```
Same art style and character/color rules as Variante 1.

WIDE SHOT of an abandoned underground subway PLATFORM seen down its length. The three figures (Nora in teal hoodie, Theo in olive bomber jacket, Schatten the black dog with amber eyes) stand SMALL in the lower third, near-center, their flashlight beams cutting down the platform into the dark.

The platform: long, curved, covered in fifty years of dust. Cracked off-white tiles. An overturned bench. A faded 1972 poster on the wall. At the far end, the dark mouth of a tunnel sealed by a crude brick wall that does NOT belong.

THE HINGUCKER: high on the wall, an old round signal bell glows cold green-white (#7fae9a) — ringing by itself. From the bricked-up tunnel mouth, a thin cold-blue mist seeps out. Schatten faces the bricked wall, leaning toward it.

GHOST HINT: extremely faint pale shape near the bricked wall — ambiguous, not a clear figure.

TITLE: "SCHATTEN SIEHT MEHR" across the upper third, same engraved chalk-white industrial capitals, lit faintly by the signal glow. Series line above, subtitle and author as in Variante 1.

Palette, lighting, 70/20/10 distribution identical to Variante 1. Steel-blue dominant, signal-green + amber accents, NO purple, NO forest green.

MOOD: a vast, sleeping, wrong place. The children are tiny intruders. Something at the far end is awake.
```

## VARIANTE 3 – "Die Murmel im Staub" (Symbolisch, minimalistisch)
### Kernidee: Ein einzelnes Objekt trägt die ganze Geschichte

Eine ruhigere, symbolische Variante (gut als eBook-Thumbnail, da auf kleiner Fläche sofort lesbar). Im Vordergrund Schattens Augen + ein einzelnes warmes Detail; der Rest Andeutung.

```
Same art style and color rules as Variante 1.

CLOSE, MOODY composition. Lower foreground: the dark silhouette of Schatten's head in profile, facing into darkness, his single visible AMBER EYE (#d4920b) glowing — sharp focus, the emotional anchor. Behind/around him, out of focus, the suggestion of a descending tiled staircase into black.

ONE SMALL WARM-COLD DETAIL on a dusty stone step, caught in a thin flashlight beam: a single glass MARBLE with a blue thread inside it (#5a8fc0), resting in the dust. Tiny, precise, lonely. (This is the heart-object of the book — readers will understand it only after reading; on the cover it simply intrigues.)

Far back: the faint cold green-white glow of the railway signal (#7fae9a) and rising pale-blue mist.

TITLE: "SCHATTEN SIEHT MEHR" upper third, engraved chalk-white industrial capitals. Series line above, subtitle + author as Variante 1.

Palette: steel-blue dominant (#0c1218 / #1a1c1f), accents amber eye + signal-green + the marble's cold blue. NO purple, NO forest green.

MOOD: quiet, eerie, intimate. The dog sees something we don't. A single marble in the dark asks a question.
```

## Iterations-Checkliste (nach der Generierung prüfen)

- [ ] **Schatten zieht HINEIN** (lehnt nach vorn/unten, Leine straff) — NICHT verweigernd. Das ist der Markenmoment.
- [ ] Schattens Augen sind **bernsteinfarben** (#d4920b), glühend — der eine warme Punkt.
- [ ] Das **Signal glüht grün-weiß** (#7fae9a) tief im Dunkel — der zweite, größere Akzent.
- [ ] Nora trägt **Teal-Hoodie**, Theo **olivgrüne Bomberjacke** (Reihen-Konsistenz).
- [ ] Gesichter NICHT voll sichtbar (von hinten/halbes Profil).
- [ ] **Kein klarer Geist** sichtbar — höchstens ambivalente Andeutung tief im Tunnel.
- [ ] Palette **stahlblau-schwarz** — KEIN Lila (B1), KEIN Waldgrün (B2).
- [ ] Titel "SCHATTEN SIEHT MEHR" lesbar, oberes Drittel, gravierte kalkweiße Industrieschrift.
- [ ] Serienzeile "DIE GEISTERSPÜRER · BAND 3" vorhanden.
- [ ] Untertitel + Autor (Benjamin Krug) vorhanden.
- [ ] Gesamteindruck: kribbelig-einladend, nie blutig/verstörend ("Kribbeln, kein Albtraum").
