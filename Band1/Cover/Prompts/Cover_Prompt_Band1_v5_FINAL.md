## VORDERSEITE (eBook)

```
Digital illustration, children's book cover art, portrait orientation 1600x2560px.

SCENE: Night. A girl (12) and a smaller boy (10) stand with their backs to the viewer, side by side, looking up at a tall old German apartment building. Between them sits a medium-sized dark dog with glowing amber eyes (#d4920b), alert, ears forward.

THE BUILDING: One single old German Altbau, 3 stories, weathered grey plaster. Takes up the center and upper half of the image. Slightly exaggerated perspective from below — the building looms.
- Ground floor windows: COMPLETELY DARK. No light.
- First floor windows: WARM golden-amber glow (#d4920b). Safety.
- Second floor: ONE window glows COLD BLUE (#c8dff5). In the condensation on that window, the word HILF is barely visible, written by a child's finger. This is the focal point.

THE CHILDREN (seen from behind, lower third of image):
- Girl (left): shoulder-length brown hair, DARK TEAL zip hoodie (#2a8a7a). Determined posture.
- Boy (right): shorter, messy dark-blond hair, OLIVE-GREEN bomber jacket. Slightly behind his sister.
- Dog (center, between them): almost black fur, sits upright, GLOWING AMBER EYES visible even from behind/side.

ATMOSPHERE:
- Wet cobblestones reflecting moonlight.
- CYOA VISUAL CUE: The cobblestone path in front of the children SPLITS INTO TWO — a fork in the road. One path leads straight to the building's front door, the other curves away into darkness to the left. This is a subtle visual metaphor for "you choose your path." Keep it natural — just a Y-shaped fork in the wet stones, not exaggerated.
- Thin fog tendrils seeping from the building's front door, which is SLIGHTLY AJAR revealing pitch darkness inside.
- Cold silver moonlight from upper left.
- Color palette: deep navy (#1a1a3e), indigo (#2d1b4e), with SATURATED amber and teal accents. NOT uniformly dark — use color contrast.

COMPOSITION:
- Upper 30% of image: dark sky, building top. THIS AREA MUST BE DARK AND RELATIVELY EMPTY — text will be placed here later.
- Lower 15%: cobblestones, children's feet. MUST BE DARK — author name goes here.
- The cold blue window is positioned in the upper-middle area — it is the eye-catcher.

CRITICAL RULES:
- ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT anywhere in the image. No title, no author, no words except HILF in the window condensation.
- NO other people, no ghosts, no monsters, no skulls, no blood.
- NO bright warm colors except the amber window and dog's eyes.
- Style: painterly digital illustration, slightly stylized (not photorealistic, not cartoon). Think Lockwood & Co. UK covers meets Goosebumps color saturation.
- The image must read clearly when scaled down to 150px width.
```

## RUCKSEITE (Taschenbuch)

```
Digital illustration, book back cover, portrait orientation 1575x2475px.

SCENE: Same night, same street, but the view is now looking AWAY from the building — down the empty cobblestone alley.

ELEMENTS:
- Left edge: old stone wall with dark ivy, an extinguished wrought-iron street lantern.
- Right edge: ornate rusted iron fence (lighter area — ISBN barcode will go here).
- Center: empty dark cobblestone alley disappearing into fog. The CENTER MUST BE A UNIFORM DARK GRADIENT — text will be overlaid here.
- Bottom: 2-3 sets of small dog paw prints in the wet cobblestones (Schatten's tracks). 1-2 dry autumn leaves.
- Sky (top 15%): night sky continuation, faint church spires silhouette. No moon.

ATMOSPHERE: same color palette as front. Deep navy, indigo, cold blue accents. Slightly lighter on right side for barcode zone.

CRITICAL RULES:
- ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT anywhere.
- Center area MUST be dark enough for white/light text overlay.
- Right lower corner MUST be light enough for a white barcode rectangle.
- NO people, no dog, no figures. Only the paw prints suggest Schatten was here.
```
