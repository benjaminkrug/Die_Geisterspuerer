# D8.6 – Final read-through (complete English text)

Source: `Band1/Manuskript_EN/The_Whispering_Window_Komplett.md`, read straight through as a reader would (Ch. 1–16). Only problems that show up across chapter boundaries or that an editor would fix before print. Ranked by importance.

Format: **Chapter · quote · problem · fix**

## A. Must fix before print

1. **Front matter (line 5)** · "> Arbeitsfassung (Übersetzung fertig geprüft, 2026-10-14) …" · A German working note sits in the book text. · Delete the line.
2. **Ch. 12 → 13** · Ch. 12 ends: "She held on tight to the diary". Ch. 13 opens: Theo is "hugging the diary to his chest. Sometime during the night, Nora had given it to him. He hadn't let go of it since." · Two lines apart, the diary changes hands, and the text explains it with a time span that doesn't exist. · Ch. 13: "Nora handed him the diary. He hugged it to his chest." (and cut "Sometime during the night… since").
3. **Ch. 3** · "That's the third time you've said that," Theo muttered. · Nora has said "There's an explanation" only twice (lines 495, 591). · Change it to "That's the second time you've said that", or add one more "There's an explanation" earlier in Ch. 3.
4. **Ch. 5 / 11 / 16 – Mrs. Silber's handwriting** · Ch. 5: "Small and scrawly" and "the same scrawly handwriting". Ch. 16: "Mrs. Silber's scrawly handwriting". Ch. 11 (her notebook): "Tight, neat, old-fashioned." · The same woman's handwriting is described two opposite ways. · Ch. 11: "Small, tight, scrawly. Blue ink." (or make it "neat" in all three places).
5. **Ch. 3 → 4 – Shadow's position and the dog bed** · Ch. 3 ends: "Shadow lay under the table, chewing on a toy bone." Ch. 4, same moment: "Shadow lay in the new dog bed next to the radiator. Mom had gotten it the night before, along with a leash." · He jumps from under the table to the dog bed with no movement in between. Also, Ch. 2 has "Tomorrow we're buying dog food… And a leash. And a dog bed," and Mom fell asleep after pizza, so she couldn't have bought them "the night before". · Ch. 4: "Shadow had crawled out from under the table. His head was up…", and either cut the dog-bed sentence or change it to "Mom had picked up a leash and a dog bed on her way to the early shift."
6. **Ch. 11 → 12 – time jump** · Ch. 11 ends with the kids inside Mrs. Silber's apartment in daylight, hearing "Thump. Thump. Thump." Ch. 12 opens with Nora waking in bed, and a single line of backfill: "That evening, the knocking had stopped before they worked up the nerve to open the door." · The reader can't tell how they got out, which door is meant, or when evening came. · Expand the backfill to two sentences: "They had stood frozen in Mrs. Silber's hallway until the knocking stopped. Then they had run downstairs, and neither of them had dared open the cold room's door."
7. **Ch. 7 → 8 → 9 → 10 → 11 – time-of-day cues** · Ch. 7: the window fogs "In the afternoon". Ch. 8: "walk through the dark apartment… Ten very long, very dark feet". Ch. 9 ends at night in mood, and Ch. 10 starts with no time marker. Ch. 11: "Daylight. Warm air. Dust in the sunbeams." · The reader loses track of whether the basement trip happens that evening or the next day. · Ch. 10, first line: "The sun was still up when they opened the basement door." In Ch. 8, change "dark" to "empty" ("the empty apartment… Ten very long, very empty feet").
8. **Ch. 13** · "Moonlight came in through the kitchen window at the end of the hall" – a few lines later: "Past the kitchen… All the way to the end of the hall… The cold room." · Two different rooms are both placed at the end of the hall (Ch. 2 also puts the kitchen early in the hallway). · Change it to "Moonlight fell through the open kitchen door and painted a silver stripe on the floorboards."
9. **Ch. 7 vs. Ch. 4 – kitchen floor and the glass** · Ch. 4: "Milk spread across the floorboards." Ch. 7: the glass "crashed onto the tiles". Also in Ch. 7, Nora only *wanted* a glass of water ("She wanted a glass of water… Then she saw the window"), so there is no glass in her hand yet. · The floor changes material, and the glass comes from nowhere. · Ch. 7: "She took a glass from the shelf. Then she saw the window." and "crashed onto the floor".
10. **Ch. 5 vs. Ch. 11 – the study furniture** · Ch. 5: "a small table… crowded with burned-down candles" plus "A small dresser… Three drawers". Ch. 11: "The burned-down candles on the desk… Nora pulled open the desk drawers." · The table and the dresser have merged into a "desk". · Ch. 11: "The burned-down candles on the little table… Nora pulled open the dresser drawers."
11. **Ch. 8 → 9 – Shadow's position** · Ch. 8 ends: "He pressed himself against Nora's leg and whined." Ch. 9, line 3: "Shadow whined next to Theo." · He switches sides across the chapter break. · Ch. 9: "Shadow whined against her leg."

## B. Repetitions visible in one sitting

12. **"like it was the most natural thing in the world"** – 3× (Ch. 1, 5, 13), each time about Shadow walking through a door. · Keep it in Ch. 1. Ch. 5: "as if he'd never refused". Ch. 13: "as if he lived here".
13. **"In July." / "In the middle of July."** as a one-beat scare tag – 6× (Ch. 2 ×2, Ch. 3, Ch. 3 morning, Ch. 15, Ch. 16), plus several "In summer." · Keep Ch. 2 (first) and Ch. 15. Cut it in Ch. 3 (both) and Ch. 16.
14. **Metallic taste on the tongue** – 5× (Ch. 1, 3 "tasted like metal", 5 "Like coins", 10 "Like old coins", 15). · Cut Ch. 10, or change it there to "The air tasted like wet stone."
15. **"No joke(s). No sarcasm."** as a full beat – 3× word-for-word (Ch. 4, 7, 9), plus "No joke this time." (Ch. 16). · Keep Ch. 9 (the strongest). Ch. 4: "He didn't make a single joke." Ch. 7: "He wasn't joking."
16. **"His hackles rose… from his neck all the way to his tail"** – 3× (Ch. 2, 11, 15). "Hackles rose" appears 7× in total. · Ch. 15: "His fur stood up in a ridge down his back."
17. **"As if written with a (child's) finger."** – 4× (Ch. 4 ×2, Ch. 7, Ch. 12), and "Then she saw the window." word-for-word in Ch. 4 and Ch. 7. · Ch. 7: "Small, careful letters." Ch. 12: cut the line ("Big, hurried letters" is enough).
18. **"the last page with writing on it"** – 3× (Ch. 9, 11, 12). · Ch. 11: "the last entry". Ch. 12: "the final pages".
19. **"hit (her) like a punch"** – Ch. 9 (first line) and Ch. 12. "like a wall" – 3× (Ch. 2 ×2, Ch. 3). · Ch. 12: "The cold was waiting for her." Ch. 3: "Like stepping into a freezer."
20. **Dropping things at the window** – the bowl crashes (Ch. 3), the glass clinks (Ch. 4), the glass crashes (Ch. 7). · Keep 3 and 4. In Ch. 7, Nora just freezes with the glass in her hand.
21. **"he/Theo could almost make her laugh"** – Ch. 3 and Ch. 5, nearly the same sentence. · Ch. 5: cut "Even here… Almost." and keep only "Nora snorted. Almost a laugh."
22. Minor (fine to keep, but watch): "white cloud" of breath 6×, "fingers tingled" 6×, "goosebumps" 5×, mouth/throat "dry" 5×, "I want it on the record" (Ch. 4, 5, two chapters in a row). The tombstone joke (Ch. 4, 12) works as a callback.

24. **Ch. 1 / Ch. 12 – nightstand** · Ch. 2: "a moving box served as her nightstand". Ch. 12: "took the diary out of her nightstand drawer". · Ch. 12: "out from under her pillow" (or "out of the box by her bed").
25. **Ch. 10** · "Old bikes." then "past the old bike". · Make it "past the bikes".
26. **Ch. 2 vs. Ch. 1** · Mom has a two-hour meeting (Ch. 1), but at dinner "Mom told them about her first day at the hospital." · Change it to "about her meeting at the hospital".

## D. Still sounds translated

28. **Ch. 2** · "It fell twenty degrees." · A US reader takes this as Fahrenheit, which is mild next to visible breath. · "It felt like it fell thirty degrees. Maybe more." (or "from summer to winter in one step").
29. **Ch. 15** · "The elementary school on the Ring." · This is the German "am Ring", and it means nothing to a US kid. · "The elementary school on Ring Road."
30. **Ch. 1** · "A sound that skipped Nora's ears. / And landed in her stomach." · "skipped" reads like a calque. · "A sound that went right past Nora's ears."
31. **Ch. 1** · "The knocking came from the wall. Right behind her headboard. Directly above her." · Wall and ceiling contradict each other. · "Right behind her headboard. From up there."
32. **Ch. 6** · "Focus. We need 1974." · They are already in the 1974 box. · "Focus. We need November."

## E. Typos / small slips

33. **Ch. 5** · "Nora moved in closer. Her eyes moved over the books." · "moved" twice. · "Her eyes ran over the books."
34. **Ch. 6** · Two paragraphs in a row are Nora's dialogue ("…With question marks." / "Whatever Mrs. Silber was doing," Nora said slowly…). · Merge them into one paragraph.
35. **Ch. 3** · After "Shadow stood up… Between Theo and the stairwell." the next paragraph opens with "She opened the apartment door". · "She" seems to point to Shadow. · "Nora opened the apartment door".
36. **Ch. 9** · "A hot stab went through her chest. Guilt. Hot and sharp." comes *after* Theo's reply and "Almost a laugh", then "Nora opened her eyes". · "Hot" is used twice, and the order is muddled. · Move the guilt line before "Theo was quiet" and change it to "Guilt. Sharp as a splinter."
37. **Ch. 16** · "She got up and went to the desk. She went to roll up the map." · "went" twice. · "She got up and went to the desk to roll up the map."

## Verdict

The text is clean and reads well. The voice is consistent, the jokes land, there are no doubled words or quote-mark problems, and almost nothing still sounds German. It is not quite ready for print: the German working note on line 5 has to go, and there are about ten continuity slips across chapter breaks (#2–#11) that an attentive young reader will notice (the diary changing hands, "third time", the handwriting, the dog bed, the time-of-day cues). Once those are fixed and the most visible repetitions are trimmed (#12–#17), the book is ready to publish as text. All fixes are line edits, with no rewriting needed.
