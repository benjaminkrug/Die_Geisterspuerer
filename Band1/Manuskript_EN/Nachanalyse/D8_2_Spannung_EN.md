**Observations**
- The book starts low (Ch1–4 average 4.75). Ch4 is the weakest early chapter: there is no danger and its ending is soft.
- Two dips after reveals: Ch7 (4) gets a quiet, emotional day after the Ch6 address reveal, and Ch13 (6) has zero danger by design (the release).
- The strongest stretch is Ch9–11 (8, 8, 9). The finale (Ch15–16: 8, 9) sets up the sequel well.
- Danger peaks at only two points (Ch10, Ch15 = 3). The emotional climax (Ch12–13) runs on F, not G, which suits the genre.
- S is consistently strong (14 of 16 chapters at 2). Only Ch4 and Ch7 end softly.
