> **Besonderheit dieses Bandes:** Schatten *verweigert* den Friedhof (Story-Outline,

> Schärfungs-Regel "Schatten prominent" zu verletzen — seine Weigerung IST die Spannung.

## MODUL 1 — HERO

```
Erzeuge ein Bild im Seitenverhältnis 3:2 (quer, ca. 1500×1000 px). Dies ist ein NEU
komponiertes Querformat-Banner, KEIN Ausschnitt des Covers. Übernimm vom angehängten
Referenz-Cover (Die Geisterspürer, Band 2) NUR: Mal-Stil, Pinselführung, Farbwelt,
Lichtstimmung und das exakte Aussehen der beiden Kinder und des Hundes. Komposition neu und quer.
Verwende diese genaue Beschreibung:

STIL: semi-realistic digital painting for a German children's horror-adventure book (ages 10-12),
painterly visible brushstrokes, cinematic moody night lighting, like a film poster. NOT a photo,
NOT 3D render, NOT cartoon, NOT manga, NOT cute or childish.

PALETTE: deep navy blue (#1a1a3e), dark indigo-violet (#2d1b4e), cool blue-grey (#3a4a6b). Cold
bluish-white (#c8dff5) for ghostly glow, fog and moonlight. ONE warm amber-gold accent (#d4920b).
Muted teal (#2a8a7a) ONLY on the girl's hoodie. Dark and atmospheric, but never pitch black.

SZENE (Querformat, neu komponiert): an old cemetery gate at night, beyond it rows of pale,
unmarked gravestones fading into thick cold fog. In the lower-LEFT, two children stand seen from
slightly BEHIND (we see their backs), just outside the gate, looking in — rendered as near-
silhouettes, gently rim-lit. Left: a slightly taller girl with shoulder-length brown hair in a
TEAL hoodie (Nora). Beside her, half a step back: a smaller boy with messy dark-blond hair in an
OLIVE-GREEN bomber jacket (Theo). Beside them, NOT entering, hackles raised, refusing to step past
the gate: a medium dark, almost black dog with luminous AMBER eyes (Schatten) — his refusal is the
tension of this image. Thin cold fog (#c8dff5) drifts low between the gravestones. A pale moon
sits upper-left, cool and dim.

LICHT-LOGIK: only ONE warm light accent (the dog's amber eyes); it is the eye's anchor against
the cold fog and moonlight beyond the gate. The moon and fog stay cool and never compete with the
amber eyes. The eye travels from the children and the reluctant dog (left) to the warm point and
up to the title.

KOMPOSITION & TEXTZONE: reserve the UPPER part of the sky (roughly the top 35%, calm, uniform and
dark) as the text zone — no busy clouds, no bright spots there, no text over the children or the glow.

TEXT (spell exactly, Wort für Wort; clean classic serif, warm cream color, high contrast over the
calm dark sky, fully inside the image, not touching any edge; achte exakt auf Umlaute ä ö ü ß):
Klein, oben, gesperrt, Großbuchstaben:  DIE GEISTERSPÜRER · BAND 2
Groß darunter, zwei Zeilen:  Der Friedhof
ohne Namen
Kleiner, eine Zeile:  Hunderte Gräber, kein einziger Name.
Unten, klein:  Gänsehaut mit Sicherheitsnetz – ab 10 Jahren.

No visible ghosts, no monsters, no scary faces, no glowing eyes (except the dog's warm amber eyes),
no blood, no modern bright objects, no neon, no cartoon or manga style. Mood: mysterious and
inviting, not horror. Spell every German word correctly.
```

## MODUL 3 — DAS TEAM + THEO

```
Erzeuge ein Bild im Seitenverhältnis 3:2 (quer, ca. 1500×1000 px). Übernimm vom angehängten
Referenz-Cover (Die Geisterspürer, Band 2) den Mal-Stil, die Farbwelt, die Lichtstimmung
und das Aussehen der beiden Kinder und des Hundes (Noras türkiser Hoodie, Theos olivgrüne Jacke,
Schattens bernsteinfarbene Augen). Auf dem Cover sieht man sie von hinten; zeige sie hier dem
Betrachter ZUGEWANDT mit Gesichtern, die zu denselben Kindern passen. Verwende diese genaue
Beschreibung:

STIL: semi-realistic digital painting, painterly brushwork, warm-but-moody. NOT a photo, NOT 3D,
NOT cartoon, NOT manga.

SZENE: the same two children and their dog, turned TOWARD the viewer in a friendly three-quarter
view so we clearly see the kids' faces, standing close together as a team just outside an old
cemetery gate at dusk, softly blurred behind them. LEFT, half a step forward, calm and determined:
a 12-year-old girl with shoulder-length brown hair in a TEAL hoodie (Nora). RIGHT, a touch behind
her but clearly part of the team, nervous yet brave, with a small wry smile: a smaller 10-year-old
boy with messy dark-blond hair in an OLIVE-GREEN bomber jacket (Theo). BESIDE them, sitting back
slightly, hackles a little raised but not aggressive, refusing to go further: a dark medium dog
with luminous AMBER eyes (Schatten). One faint cool glow (fog over distant gravestones) sits far
behind them.

GESICHTER (wichtig): the two children's faces are those of real 10–12-year-olds — natural child
proportions, lively warm eyes (no empty or staring eyes), gentle natural expressions, friendly and
approachable. Soft warm key light on their faces from the front-left so features read clearly.
Do NOT make them look older, creepy, or doll-like.

LICHT-LOGIK: the main warm light is a soft glow on the children's faces and the dog's amber eyes
(the focal point). The background fog-glow stays cool, dim and secondary and must not compete.

KOMPOSITION & TEXTZONE: keep the two children and the dog together in the CENTER third. Reserve a
calm, darker band across the TOP (~20%) for the heading and a calm darker band across the BOTTOM
(~25%) for the lower text — no busy detail in those bands.

TEXT (spell exactly, Wort für Wort; clean classic serif, warm cream, high contrast over the calm
top/bottom bands, fully inside, not touching edges, not overlapping the faces; achte exakt auf
Umlaute ä ö ü ß):
Groß, oben:  Zwei Geschwister. Ein Hund. Ein Geheimnis.
Unten, kleiner, ein kurzer Block:
  Nora bleibt ruhig. Schatten warnt immer vorher. Und Theo?
  Theo hat vor allem Angst – und genau er hat die rettende Idee.
  Mut heißt nicht, keine Angst zu haben.

No visible ghosts, no monsters, no scary faces, no glowing eyes (except the dog's warm amber eyes),
no blood, no neon, no cartoon or manga style. Keep both children's faces friendly and warm. Spell
every German word correctly.
```
