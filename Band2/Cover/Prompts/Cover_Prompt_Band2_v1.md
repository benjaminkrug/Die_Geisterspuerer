# Cover-Prompts – Band 2: Der Friedhof ohne Namen (3 Varianten)

## Design-Philosophie Band 2

| Regel | Umsetzung |
|-------|-----------|
| **70% Hintergrund** | Dunkler Friedhof, Bäume, Nebel, Nachthimmel — Waldgrün-Schwarz-Grau-Palette |
| **20% Überschrift** | "DER FRIEDHOF OHNE NAMEN" — Gothic-Gravur-Optik, knochengrau, ~40% Bildhöhe |
| **10% Hingucker** | EIN warmer Akzent je Variante (Augen / glühender Stein / Brenners Licht) |
| **Differenzierung B1** | B1 = Altbau + Indigoblau-Lila → B2 = Friedhof + Waldgrün-Schwarz |
| **Typografie** | Teil der Komposition — nicht nachträglich. Breite Textzone in der Bildmitte |

## VARIANTE 1 – "Das Tor"
### Kernidee: Spannung durch Verweigerung

Schatten weigert sich einzutreten. Das ist das erste Bild, das der Leser sieht — und er weiß sofort: dahinter ist etwas.

```
Children's book cover illustration in a modern, semi-realistic digital painting style with slight cartoon proportions. Painterly visible brushstrokes, rich textures, and cinematic lighting. The style should feel like a movie poster for a middle-grade horror-adventure film for ages 10-12. NOT chibi, NOT manga, NOT flat-color cartoon, NOT photorealistic, NOT cute or childish.

IMPORTANT — CAMERA ANGLE: The entire scene is viewed from a SLIGHTLY LOW ANGLE, looking UPWARD. The iron gate towers above the children. The viewer crouches on the path, looking up. This makes the gate imposing and the children appear small but brave. This low perspective is critical for the dramatic effect.

════════════════════════════════════════
TYPOGRAPHY ZONE (center of image — CRITICAL)
════════════════════════════════════════

The TITLE occupies approximately 40% of the total image HEIGHT, positioned in the CENTER BAND of the image (roughly 20% to 60% from top). This is the visual centerpiece.

TITLE TEXT: "DER FRIEDHOF" on one line, "OHNE NAMEN" on the second line.
- Font style: Gothic stone-carving aesthetic — letters appear CHISELED INTO DARK STONE, with slight weathering cracks at the edges. NOT digital-clean, NOT serif, NOT handwritten. Think: letters carved into a medieval gravestone, slightly eroded.
- Letter color: Bone-gray-white (#e8e0d0) with a very subtle warm yellowing toward the edges, as if old parchment or aged marble. A faint dark outline/shadow gives depth.
- Decorative elements: At each end of both title lines, thin ivy or thorn tendrils curl organically from the letter tips. These tendrils are very thin, dark green-gray, and spread NO more than 15% of image width to each side.
- The title has NO glow, NO neon, NO modern effects. It is heavy, stone-like, and slightly oppressive.

SERIES TITLE: "Die Geisterspürer" — positioned ABOVE the main title, smaller (roughly 6% image height). Elegant thin serif font, slightly faded bone-white (#d8d0c0), centered.

AGE BADGE: Small circular stamp in the LOWER LEFT corner — diameter roughly 8% of image width. Dark forest green background (#1a2e1a), cream text "Ab 10 Jahren" in a classic serif font. Vintage ink-stamp texture. Subtle, not cartoonish.

════════════════════════════════════════
FOREGROUND (bottom 30% of image, in front of and beside the gate)
════════════════════════════════════════

Three figures stand on an old cobblestone path leading to the iron gate. They face AWAY from the viewer, looking at the gate. Their backs and partial profiles are visible — NOT their full faces.

LEFT — NORA (girl, 12 years old):
- Slightly taller of the two children
- Shoulder-length straight brown hair, one hand extended, fingers nearly touching the iron gate
- Wearing a dark TEAL zip-up hoodie (#2a8a7a) — her hoodie is the MOST SATURATED non-accent color in the foreground
- Dark jeans, worn sneakers
- Posture: determined, one step ahead, shoulders squared — she is the leader

RIGHT — THEO (boy, 10 years old):
- Shorter than Nora by about a head
- Messy, slightly curly dark-blond hair
- Wearing an oversized OLIVE-GREEN BOMBER JACKET (military olive green — NOT brown, NOT dark brown)
- Dark cargo pants, worn-out sneakers
- One hand gripping the strap of his backpack, body turned slightly sideways — part of him wants to run
- He stands half a step BEHIND Nora

CENTER-FRONT (between the children, partially in front) — SCHATTEN (the dog):
- Medium-sized mixed-breed dog with dark, almost black fur
- LUMINOUS AMBER-COLORED EYES (#d4920b) — THIS IS THE PRIMARY HINGUCKER OF THIS COMPOSITION. The eyes are the ONLY warm, saturated color in the image. They glow faintly from within, like firelight in the dark.
- Schatten is SITTING, completely still. He faces the gate. His hackles (fur along spine) are RAISED — a visible ridge of fur. He does NOT lean forward. He refuses.
- One subtle detail: his ears are back, not forward. He is not curious. He is warning.

════════════════════════════════════════
MIDDLE GROUND (center of image) — THE IRON GATE
════════════════════════════════════════

A tall, ornate WROUGHT IRON GATE fills the center vertical strip of the image. This is the entrance to Sektion C of the old cemetery.

Gate design: Gothic iron bars, pointed tips, decorative swirls at the intersections. Slightly rusted (dark brown-rust tones at joints and tips). The gate is CLOSED. Through the bars, the cemetery interior is visible.

On the iron arch above the gate, a metal sign (barely legible): "SEKTION C" — old, weathered lettering.

THROUGH THE GATE (visible between the bars):
- Rows of weathered gravestones — ALL NAMELESS. No text visible on any stone. Just smooth, eroded surfaces.
- Thick white-gray fog hangs low between the stones, knee-high to where a person would stand

FAR BACK, at the very edge of visibility, barely distinguishable from the fog:
- A small, HUNCHED GRAY FIGURE — Friedrich Brenner. He is translucent, barely there. His outline is visible only because the fog is slightly less dense where he stands. He looks in the direction of the children. He is NOT threatening — he is small, still, and waiting.
- His presence should trigger "Wait... is that someone there?" — NOT "a ghost is standing there."

════════════════════════════════════════
BACKGROUND (top 30% of image)
════════════════════════════════════════

SKY: Deep dark gradient — dark forest-black (#0d1a0e) at tree-line level transitioning to dark charcoal (#1a1a1a) with a hint of deep midnight blue at the very top. NO purple, NO indigo — this differentiates from Band 1.

MOON: A large pale full moon, UPPER RIGHT this time (Band 1 had upper left). Partially obscured by the bare branches of an old tree that reaches across the upper right corner. The moon casts cold silver light on the gate and the foreground cobblestones.

TREES: Old, bare or sparse-leafed trees rise on both sides of the gate, their branches reaching upward and partially obscuring the sky. Dark, gnarled trunks. Moss on the lower bark.

ATMOSPHERE: The upper center area (behind the title zone) shows the dark tree canopy and sky — relatively uniform dark tones that allow the bone-gray title text to be readable against them.

════════════════════════════════════════
LIGHTING
════════════════════════════════════════

1. PRIMARY: Full moon (upper right) — cold silver-blue light from above-right. Catches the top edges of the gate and one side of the children's silhouettes.
2. SECONDARY: No window glow (this is outdoors). Instead: the fog in the cemetery has a faint cold internal luminescence — slightly lighter than the darkness, as if the moonlight pools in it.
3. ACCENT — HINGUCKER: Schatten's amber eyes (#d4920b) — tiny but the ONLY warm light points in the entire image. The eye draws immediately to them.
4. GHOST HINT: Brenner's area has the fog slightly lighter — barely perceptible.

Overall: Dark, atmospheric, cold. NOT pitch black — every element visible. The gate, the children, the dog are clearly readable. The fog beyond is mysterious.

════════════════════════════════════════
COLOR PALETTE — 70/20/10 DISTRIBUTION
════════════════════════════════════════

DOMINANT (70% — background atmosphere):
- Deep forest black-green (#0d1a0e)
- Dark charcoal gray (#1a1a1a)
- Fog white-gray (#c8c8c0)
- Iron gate rust-brown (#3d2b1f)
- Mossy stone gray (#4a4a3a)

TYPOGRAPHIC (20% — title zone):
- Bone-gray-white (#e8e0d0) — title letters
- Deep shadow-black (#0a0a0a) — letter depth/shadow
- Dark green-gray (#2a3a2a) — ivy tendril decoration

HINGUCKER (10% — single accent):
- Warm amber-gold (#d4920b) — ONLY on Schatten's eyes
- This is the ONLY warm, saturated color in the entire image

ABSOLUTELY FORBIDDEN: bright red, neon of any color, hot pink, orange, high-saturation warm tones, indigo, purple (to differentiate from Band 1), any color that looks modern or digital.

════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT TO AVOID
════════════════════════════════════════
```

## VARIANTE 2 – "Der Stein"

Ein leuchtendes Objekt mitten im Dunkel. Der Leser fragt: Was ist das? Und wer ist die riesige Gestalt hinten?


## VARIANTE 3 – "Zwei Geister"
### Kernidee: Epik durch Kontrast

Zwei Geister, zwei Welten. Klein vs. Groß. Kalt vs. Warm. Die Kinder stehen dazwischen.

```
Children's book cover illustration in a modern, semi-realistic digital painting style with slight cartoon proportions. Painterly visible brushstrokes, rich textures, and cinematic lighting. Middle-grade horror-adventure film aesthetic for ages 10-12. Epic scale composition. NOT chibi, NOT manga, NOT flat-color cartoon, NOT photorealistic, NOT cute or childish.

IMPORTANT — CAMERA ANGLE: WIDE, slightly elevated — the viewer stands far enough back to see the full scene. This is a THEATRICAL COMPOSITION: the two children are small in the lower foreground, flanked by two enormous ghost presences that tower above them. Think: movie poster, full-scene reveal.

════════════════════════════════════════
TYPOGRAPHY ZONE (center horizontal band — CRITICAL)
════════════════════════════════════════

The title occupies the CENTER BAND of the image, visually splitting the composition between the ghost presences above and the children below.

TITLE TEXT: "DER FRIEDHOF" on one line, "OHNE NAMEN" on a second line.
Positioned: CENTER of image, approximately 35% to 65% from top (roughly 30% of image height for the title block, with generous spacing).

- Font style: Monumental stone-carving. The letters are LARGE — they divide the image like an inscription on a massive mausoleum wall. The background visible THROUGH and AROUND the letters is the cemetery at night.
- Letter color: Bone-gray-white (#e8e0d0). The letters have a three-dimensional quality — as if they project slightly from the surface. Deep shadow on the lower edge of each letter.
- IMPORTANT: The title effectively creates a horizontal barrier between the ghost realm (above) and the children's realm (below). This is intentional and compositional.
- Decorative: At the bottom of the title block, a thin line of crumbling stone texture — as if the "wall" the letters are carved on has a bottom edge. Small fragments of stone appear to have fallen away.

SERIES TITLE: "Die Geisterspürer" — above the main title, small (6% image height). Elegant faded serif, bone-white.

AGE BADGE: Lower RIGHT corner. Circular vintage stamp, dark forest green (#1a2e1a), cream text "Ab 10 Jahren".

════════════════════════════════════════
FOREGROUND (bottom 30% of image — BELOW the title)
════════════════════════════════════════

Two children and a dog seen from BEHIND at slight distance, standing in a wide cobblestone cemetery path. They are centered, facing away from the viewer — looking up at the scene above.

NORA (left, slightly taller): Dark TEAL hoodie (#2a8a7a), dark jeans. One arm extended outward — not pointing, more like holding her ground. Her posture is rigid, determined.

THEO (right, shorter): OLIVE-GREEN BOMBER JACKET (military olive — NOT brown). Slightly crouched — not hiding, but instinctively lower. One hand at his side, fingers spread.

SCHATTEN (center, between them): Standing, alert. NOT sitting. All four legs planted. His AMBER EYES (#d4920b) face directly forward — toward the ghost confrontation above. His hackles are raised. He stands between the children and what is above, as if placing himself in the way.

The cobblestone path extends forward from them into fog.

════════════════════════════════════════
MIDDLE GROUND (center — the ghost confrontation, partially overlapping title)
════════════════════════════════════════

Two ghost presences FACE EACH OTHER across the center of the image. They are large — each at least twice the height of a normal person as rendered. They occupy the LEFT and RIGHT of the middle zone.

*** LEFT — FRIEDRICH BRENNER ***
- Small for a ghost — he hunches, making himself even smaller. He is perhaps 1.5x the height of the children (not towering, just taller).
- Form: TRANSLUCENT pale-gray (#d0cfc8) with a very faint warm tinge — not quite golden, but warmer than cold. You can see the cemetery behind him through his form. His edges blur into the fog.
- Shape: A stooped old man in worn work clothes. His hands are visible — worker's hands. His head is lowered slightly, as if exhausted.
- He faces VOSS but does not advance toward him. He holds his ground.
- Positioning: LEFT side of image, emerging from the fog between gravestones.
- His presence is NOT threatening — it is dignified and sad. He is the one who has been wronged.

*** RIGHT — HEINRICH VOSS ***
- Massive. He towers where Brenner does not. He is 2.5-3x the apparent height of the children. Broad-shouldered. His form fills the right side of the image.

- Shape: A large man in a formal coat — a city official from the 1870s. His face is OBSCURED — either turned away, in deep shadow, or his features are blurred. He radiates authority and threat, but he is still recognizably a man, not a monster.
- He faces Brenner from the RIGHT side of the image.
- His silhouette is the DARKEST element in the image — darker than the night sky behind him.

The two ghost presences FLANK the title text — the title sits between them, as if the words name the place where they are trapped.

════════════════════════════════════════
BACKGROUND (top 25% — above the ghost presences)
════════════════════════════════════════

Old cemetery trees and night sky. Ancient tree trunks rise on both sides, their branches spreading across the top of the image. The sky between branches shows a deep charcoal gradient (#1a1a1a to #0d1a0e).

Full moon: upper CENTER (rare placement — directly behind the confrontation zone, slightly obscured by branches). It casts cold light downward onto both ghost forms, creating their outlines from above.
```

## ITERATIVES VERFEINERN (alle Varianten)

Nach erster Generation in ChatGPT — Prüfliste:

### Runde 1 — Typografie
> Prüfe: (1) Nimmt der Titel ungefähr 35-40% der Bildhöhe ein? (2) Wirken die Buchstaben wie in Stein gemeißelt — verwittert, schwer, organisch? (3) Ist "Die Geisterspürer" als kleiner Serientitel über dem Haupttitel lesbar? (4) Ist der Alters-Badge als kleiner runder Stempel sichtbar?

### Runde 2 — Hingucker-Überprüfung
> Prüfe: (1) Gibt es GENAU EINEN stark leuchtenden Warm-Akzent im Bild? (2) Ist der Rest des Bildes konsequent dunkel und entsättigt? (3) Zieht der Hingucker den Blick sofort an — noch bevor man den Titel liest?

### Runde 3 — Figuren
> Prüfe: (1) Ist Noras Hoodie klar türkis-blaugrün (#2a8a7a) und nicht braun/grün/blau? (2) Ist Theos Jacke olivgrün (nicht braun, nicht khaki)? (3) Ist Schatten deutlich ein dunkler Hund mit bernsteingelben Augen? (4) Sind alle Figuren von hinten oder im Halbprofil — keine Frontalgesichter?

### Runde 4 — Palette und Differenzierung
> Prüfe: (1) Ist kein Indigo oder Lila im Bild? (Die Geisterspürer Band 1 hat diese Farben — Band 2 muss sich differenzieren.) (2) Dominieren Dunkelgrün, Schwarz und Nebel-Weiß-Grau? (3) Wirkt das Bild anders als ein Band 1 Cover, obwohl dieselben Charaktere abgebildet sind?

### Runde 5 — Gesamtstimmung
> Prüfe: (1) Löst das Cover die Frage aus: "Was ist dahinter?" oder "Was passiert hier?" — ein aktiver Sog? (2) Wirkt das Bild traurig-bedrohlich (nicht rein böse, nicht harmlos)? (3) Ist es für Kinder ab 10 einschüchternd genug — aber nicht albtraumauslösend?
