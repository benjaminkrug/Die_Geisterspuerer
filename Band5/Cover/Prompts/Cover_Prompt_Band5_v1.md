## Design-Philosophie Band 5

| Regel | Umsetzung |
|-------|-----------|
| **70% Hintergrund** | Uraltes Steingewölbe unter dem Gravenhaus — **warmes Dunkelbraun/Ruß-Schwarz**, roher Bruchstein, „älter als alles darüber" (K10) |
| **20% Überschrift** | „DER SCHLEIER" — schwere gravierte Versalien, kalkweiß. **Kurzer Titel = erstmals kein Zeilenumbruch-Problem** (vgl. B4 „TÜR") |
| **10% Hingucker** | **★ ERSTMALS KALT:** das tote silbergraue Leuchten des Spiegels — und Schattens bernsteinfarbene Augen als **einziger warmer Punkt im ganzen Bild** |
| **Differenzierung B1–B4** | B1 = Altbau + Indigo/Lila · B2 = Friedhof + Waldgrün · B3 = U-Bahn + Stahlblau · B4 = kaltes Zimmer + **warmes Gold** → **B5 = warmer Steinkeller + kaltes Silbergrau (Umkehr!)** |
| **★ Die Akzent-Umkehr** | Vier Bände lang war der Akzent **warm** (Bernstein, Signalgrün, Türgold). Band 5 dreht es um: Das Unheimliche ist jetzt **kalt**, und das einzig Warme sind die Augen des Hundes. Das ist der Kanon — Schatten ist das lebende Ding, der Anker. |
| **Serien-Markenmoment** | **★ Erstmals hat Schatten keine Wahl.** B1/B2: er verweigert. B3: er zieht erstmals hinein. B4: er zieht ins Warme. **B5: er wird GEZOGEN — er kämpft und verliert.** Krallen ziehen vier helle Linien über den Stein (K16 wörtlich). Etwas will *ihn*. |
| **★ Kein klarer Geist — trotzdem Graven** | Serienregel gewahrt: Graven ist eine **solide schwarze Silhouette von hinten**, kein Gesicht, keine Geister-Optik. Gerade das ist Kanon: *„Er war nicht durchsichtig. Man hätte ihn anfassen können — und genau das war das Falsche daran. Alle anderen verblassten. Dieser hier weigerte sich."* (K10) Nach vier Bänden vager Andeutungen steht da endlich **etwas Festes**. |
| **Vertikale Staffelung** | Titel oben (30%) → Spiegel + Graven Mitte (38–60%) → Schatten/Kinder unten (64–100%). Titel & Spiegel überlappen NIE. |
| **Thumbnail-Anker** | Zwei Bernstein-Augen + die hohe helle Spiegelfläche + der Titel. **Genau drei Elemente** — bewusst nicht mehr. |
| **Ton** | „Kribbeln, kein Albtraum" — kein Blut, keine Fratze. Die Frage, nicht der Schock: *Warum dreht der Mann sich nicht um?* |

SETTING (70%) — THE ANCIENT STONE VAULT
════════════════════════════════════════

An underground vault of rough, hand-cut stone, far older than any building above it. Low arches, heavy pillars, a floor of worn flagstones. The stone is WARM in tone — dark browns, soot-blacks, ochre-tinged greys, lit only by the mirror. Dust hangs in the air. No windows, no candles, no other light source. ════════════════════════════════════════

THE MIRROR — ‼️ THE LARGEST AND BRIGHTEST SHAPE ON THE COVER. Taller than a person, set in a heavy, ornate BLACK frame, standing upright on the flagstones, CENTERED and fully visible — nothing overlaps it.

- ‼️ ITS SURFACE REFLECTS ABSOLUTELY NOTHING. No room, no vault, no people, no dog, no man — not even a distorted or darkened reflection. It is a flat, dead, evenly lit sheet of pale SILVER-GREY, like standing water on an overcast day. Perfectly featureless. Do NOT paint any image, scene, figure, child, face, or shape inside the mirror. Its emptiness is the entire point and the cover's central unease: a mirror that refuses to reflect.
- It glows faintly from within with a cold, flat, colourless light — the brightest thing in the picture, but not blinding, not beautiful. Sickly and calm.
- Thin cold mist curls low around its base where the pale light meets the warm stone floor.

THE MAN (Alwin Graven) — a TALL SOLID BLACK SILHOUETTE standing BESIDE the mirror, at its edge, NOT in front of it. ‼️ He must NOT cover or overlap the glowing surface — the whole empty grey sheet stays visible to the viewer. He is the thing that refuses to see what WE can see. Seen from BEHIND and slightly to the side. Clearly SECONDARY to the mirror in visual weight — narrower, quieter, further back.
- ‼️ NO FACE, NO FACIAL FEATURES, NO EYES — he is a clean, solid, opaque silhouette. He is NOT transparent, NOT ghostly, NOT glowing, NOT ragged, NOT skeletal, NOT a monster. He is simply, completely SOLID — a real man-shape cut out of the light. That solidity is the horror: everything else in this world fades, and he refuses to.
- Long, old-fashioned, neatly buttoned overcoat reaching below the knee, high collar, perfectly upright posture, arms still. He looks like a polite gentleman from two centuries ago.
- ‼️ THE KEY POSE — THIS IS THE STORY OF THE COVER, GET THIS EXACTLY RIGHT:
  He stands SIDEWAYS to the mirror, shoulders square to the viewer's left, his BODY FACING OUT
  ACROSS THE VAULT — away from the glass, toward the far dark archway on the opposite side of
  the room. His face and gaze are aimed at that far archway, level and calm, chin up.
  His NEAREST ARM reaches BACKWARD, behind his own body, and his hand rests on the edge of the
  mirror's frame — an over-the-shoulder, backhanded touch, the relaxed way someone reaches back
  for a door handle they have known for twenty years without looking.
  Think of a man standing at a bus stop with one hand resting back on the pole behind him: his
  attention is entirely elsewhere. The mirror is BEHIND HIS SHOULDER. He is touching it and his
  eyes are pointed at the far wall.

  distant archway across the vault.)
- He casts a real, hard, dark shadow across the flagstones — the only thing in the vault that does.
- Keep him a clean readable silhouette: no clutter, no detail on the coat.

FOREGROUND (~64–100%) — THE DOG (dominant) + THE TWO CHILDREN
════════════════════════════════════════

‼️ ALL THREE ARE SEEN FROM DIRECTLY BEHIND — the viewer looks over their shoulders at the mirror, exactly as if standing behind them in the room. We see the BACK OF NORA'S HEAD and the BACK OF THEO'S HEAD. Their faces are simply not in the picture — not in profile, not in three-quarter view, not glimpsed. (This is a fixed rule across the whole series: the reader never sees the children's faces.)

They are NOT flat black silhouettes: they are fully painted figures, lit from the front by the mirror, so their clothing colours read clearly — but always from behind. The DOG is the largest, most central foreground shape.

SCHATTEN (the dog) — THE MAIN FOREGROUND FIGURE:
- Medium-sized shaggy mixed-breed dog with dark, almost-black fur, seen from behind and slightly to the side, low to the ground.
- ‼️ A simple narrow LEATHER COLLAR around his neck — a plain strap, nothing else. He wears NO harness, NO chest straps, NO vest, NO body rig.
- LUMINOUS AMBER EYE (#d4920b) — his head is turned in profile toward the mirror, so ONE amber eye is visible, glowing from within. ‼️ This is the ONLY WARM COLOUR IN THE ENTIRE IMAGE and the cover's thumbnail anchor: it must stay clearly visible and warm even when the cover is tiny. Everything else is cold grey light or dark stone; this one point is ALIVE.
- ‼️ CRITICAL POSTURE — THE SERIES BEAT, AND IT IS NEW: Schatten is NOT walking toward the mirror and NOT refusing. He is being DRAGGED toward it by something invisible, and he is LOSING. All four legs braced and skidding, body low and twisted back against the pull, head straining away from the glass, ears back. His CLAWS SCRAPE FOUR BRIGHT PALE LINES into the stone floor behind him — visible, fresh scratch marks in the flagstones. For the first time in the series, it is not his choice.

NORA (girl, 12) — LEFT, low behind the dog. ‼️ Seen from directly behind: the viewer sees the back of her head.
- Straight, shoulder-length MID-BROWN hair, tied back or loose — brown, not red, not ginger, not blonde.
- ‼️ DARK TEAL zip-up hoodie (#2a8a7a) — a clear blue-green teal. This is HER fixed series colour and must be plainly visible. She never wears olive, khaki, brown or blue.
- ‼️ Both hands gripping Schatten's leather COLLAR, arms straight and taut, heels dug into the stone, her whole body leaning back as a counterweight. She is holding him with everything she has — and being pulled along with him.

THEO (boy, 10) — RIGHT, half a step behind, smallest figure. ‼️ Seen from directly behind: the viewer sees the back of his head.
- Messy, slightly curly DARK-BLOND hair — dark blond, not red, not ginger, not brown.
- ‼️ Oversized MILITARY OLIVE-GREEN BOMBER JACKET — a dull yellow-green olive. This is HIS fixed series colour and must be plainly visible. He never wears blue, teal, brown or grey.
- Reaching forward toward his sister, one hand out — too far away to help yet.

‼️ DO NOT SWAP THESE TWO COLOURS: the GIRL wears TEAL (blue-green), the BOY wears OLIVE (yellow-green). Readers of the series identify them by exactly this.

The mirror is the only light in the room — nobody carries a flashlight or a lamp on this cover.

TOP ZONE (upper ~30%): the dark vaulted stone ceiling — relatively uniform dark warm-brown/black so the title is readable. ALL title text stays within this top ~30% band; keep a dark buffer between the title's bottom edge and the top of the mirror below.

SERIES LINE (small, at the very top):
"DIE GEISTERSPÜRER · BAND 5"
— thin, widely spaced capitals, cold steel-grey (#9aa6b0). Correct umlaut: Ü.

MAIN TITLE (large, clearly the dominant text):
"DER SCHLEIER"
- Heavy, engraved/carved capitals, slightly condensed, chalk-bone-white (#e8e6e0) with a hard dark shadow for depth — as if chiselled into the old stone.
- Set on TWO centered lines: "DER" small on the upper line, "SCHLEIER" large and dominant on the lower line, filling most of the cover width. (The title is short — this should be easy to balance.)
- The entire title block stays within the top ~28–30%. It must NOT extend down into or over the mirror.
- The LOWER edges of the letters catch a faint COLD SILVER rim-light from the mirror below, tying the title to the glass.
- One subtle detail: a hairline crack runs through one letter, echoing the old stone.

SUBTITLE (small, directly below the title, with a comfortable gap):

- Leave a clear gap of dark stone between the subtitle and the top of the mirror.
- PRIORITY: the MAIN TITLE stays dominant; the subtitle is small and unobtrusive.

AUTHOR (bottom edge, centered):
"Benjamin Krug"
— small centered capitals or title-case, light grey.

Keep the LOWER-RIGHT corner visually calm and free of important detail or text (a print barcode is added there later — no artwork focal points or lettering in the bottom-right ~5x3 cm).

════════════════════════════════════════
LIGHTING
════════════════════════════════════════

‼️ SINGLE-SOURCE LIGHTING — build the whole image as if the mirror were the only lamp in a sealed cellar at midnight:
1. PRIMARY — HINGUCKER: ALL light in this picture originates at the MIRROR and travels outward from it. Cold, flat, colourless silver-grey (#b8c4cc). Everything is lit only to the degree that the mirror reaches it: bright on the flagstones directly in front of the glass, dimmer on the dog and the children, fading to near-black at the edges of the frame and in the far corners. The pillars, arches and side walls FALL AWAY INTO DARKNESS the further they are from the mirror — they are lit from the mirror's direction only, never from above, never from behind the viewer, never from off-frame. Trace every highlight back to the glass. It does not flicker, and it warms nothing it touches.
2. ACCENT — THE ONLY WARMTH: Schatten's amber eye (#d4920b). One small living point against everything else.
3. The warm-toned stone catches this cold light and refuses it — the vault stays dark, its warmth living only in the shadows.
4. The room contains no candles, no torches, no lanterns, no fire, no flashlight and no window.

Overall: a sealed stone room, a light that gives no warmth, a man who will not turn around, and a dog who cannot get away. NOT pitch black — every foreground element clearly readable. Quiet and wrong, never gory.

## ★ NACHBESSERUNG von Versuch 2 (am selben Bild, NICHT neu generieren)

Versuch 2 sitzt kompositorisch. Drei Restpunkte, absteigend nach Wichtigkeit. Als
**Edit/Inpaint-Anweisung** auf das vorhandene Bild geben (ChatGPT: „Bearbeite dieses Bild,
ändere nur Folgendes …"). **Punkt 1 ist Pflicht, Punkt 2–3 sind Kür.**

**Prompt für die Nachbesserung (englisch, kopierfertig):**

```
Keep this exact image — same composition, same mirror, same man with his back turned, same
title text, same faces-away poses, same lighting and mood. Change ONLY the following:

1. THE TWO CHILDREN'S CLOTHING COLOURS (most important). Right now both are dull brown and
   blend into the dark. Repaint the GIRL on the left so her hoodie is a clear DARK TEAL
   (blue-green, #2a8a7a), and the BOY on the right so his jacket is a MILITARY OLIVE-GREEN
   (yellow-green, #6b7a3a). Let the pale mirror light catch the front edges of both garments
   so the two colours read clearly even though the children are seen from behind. These two
   colours must be the only cool/coloured notes in the picture — do not recolour anything else.

2. THE DOG. Give him a plain narrow LEATHER COLLAR (not a leash clipped to a harness), and
   let the girl's two hands grip that collar. Turn his head just enough that ONE amber-gold
   eye (#d4920b) catches the light and glows — it should be the single warm point on the
   whole cover. Add a few faint pale claw-scratch lines on the stone floor just behind his
   braced back paws, as if he is being pulled toward the mirror and resisting.

3. Leave everything else untouched.
```

**Warum Punkt 1 nicht verhandelbar ist:** Teal (Nora) und Oliv (Theo) sind die
Wiedererkennungs-Farben über Band 1–4 **und** die einzigen Farbakzente in einem sonst fast
monochrom braunen Bild. Ohne sie bricht die Serienkonsistenz im Regal.

**Fallback:** Falls die Edit-Funktion das Bild verschlechtert (Gesichter tauchen auf, Spiegel
bekommt Spiegelung), lieber das aktuelle Bild behalten und die Farben in **Canva/Affinity**
per Farbüberlagerung auf die Kleidung legen — die Silhouetten sind klar abgegrenzt, das geht
in wenigen Minuten und ohne Risiko fürs restliche Bild.

## ★ Befund aus dem 1. echten Generierungsversuch (2026-07-17)

Der erste Versuch lieferte ein handwerklich starkes Bild — **aber mit dem Reihennamen „DIE
HERRENHAUS-DETEKTIVE", dem Titel „Das Geheimnis des Spiegels", einem Herrenhaus im Kopf und
einem „KRUG VERLAG"-Logo unten rechts.** Dazu: Graven sah in den Spiegel, Nora trug Oliv statt
Teal, Theo Blau statt Oliv, beide rotblond, Noras Gesicht im Profil, Geschirr statt Halsband,
warm ausgeleuchtete Säulen.

**Was funktioniert hat — und das war das Schwerste:** der **leere Spiegel** (tote graue Fläche,
keine Spiegelung), die **vertikale Staffelung**, **Graven seitlich am Rahmen mit der Hand
darauf**, Nebel am Sockel, die gravierte Titel-Typografie.

**Die Ursachen lagen im Prompt, nicht am Generator** — drei echte Konstruktionsfehler, jetzt behoben:

| Ursache | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Die Cover-Texte standen erst bei 47 % des Prompts.** Ein Modell mit konkurrierendem Kontext greift auf den eigenen zurück, bevor es so weit liest. | **Harter Text-Block ganz oben**, der jeden früheren Kontext explizit überschreibt („NICHT Herrenhaus-Detektive"), inkl. > ⚠️ **Drei Stellen, an denen der Generator erfahrungsgemäß kämpft — in dieser Reihenfolge angehen:**
>
> **(1) Der leere Spiegel — das größte Risiko.** Bildmodelle *wollen* Spiegel spiegeln; „mirror"
> triggert den Reflex im Modell selbst. Falls er hartnäckig eine Spiegelung malt, das Wort
> ersetzen: *„a tall dark doorway of flat, featureless grey light set in a heavy black frame"*.
> **Ein Spiegel mit Spiegelung ist ein unbrauchbares Cover** — dann ist die Idee weg.
>
> **(2) Gravens Pose.** Der Generator wird ihn in den Spiegel schauen lassen wollen, weil das
> „natürlicher" aussieht. Genau das zerstört das Bild. Falls er nach ~6 Versuchen nicht
> wegschaut → **Variante B** (unten), nicht weiterwürfeln.
>
> **(3) Vier Figuren.** Zwei Gruppen (Graven Mitte, drei unten) sind mehr als bei B1–B4. Falls
> das Bild unruhig wird: Graven kleiner und weiter zurück, Schatten größer — oder Variante B.
>
> **Titel notfalls in Canva/Affinity setzen** (Bild ohne Titel generieren, obere Zone frei) —
> „DER SCHLEIER" ist kurz und umlautfrei, das sollte diesmal aber gutgehen.

## ★ VARIANTE B — ohne Graven (die konservative Fassung)

**Warum es diese Variante gibt.** Variante A bricht bewusst eine **harte Serienregel**: Band 1–4
zeigen *nie* eine klare Geistergestalt („Kein klarer Geist" steht in jeder Checkliste). Das ist
kein Zufall — die Regel schützt zwei Dinge: die Zurückhaltung der Reihe („Kribbeln, kein
Albtraum") und die Tatsache, dass Bildgeneratoren Geister zuverlässig schlecht rendern.

**Das Argument FÜR Graven (Variante A):** Er ist kein Geist im Serien-Sinn. Kanon K10:
*„Er war nicht durchsichtig. Man hätte ihn anfassen können — und genau das war das Falsche
daran. Alle anderen verblassten. Dieser hier weigerte sich."* Nach vier Bänden vager
Andeutungen endlich **etwas Festes** zu zeigen, ist die Eskalation, die ein Finale mit Grusel
8/10 verlangt. Als reine Silhouette bricht es die „keine Gesichter"-Regel nicht.

**Das Argument DAGEGEN:** Zwei Figurengruppen statt einer. Vier Figuren statt drei. Und die
ganze Wirkung hängt an einer Pose, die ein Generator missverstehen kann (er *wird* Graven in
den Spiegel schauen lassen wollen).

**→ Empfehlung: A generieren, B als Rückfallebene.** Wenn A nach ~6 Versuchen nicht sitzt
(Graven schaut hin, oder das Bild wird unruhig), ist B kein Kompromiss, sondern ein sauberes
Cover.

### Änderungen für Variante B

Im Haupt-Prompt den kompletten Block **„THE MAN (Alwin Graven)"** streichen und ersetzen durch:

```
BESIDE THE MIRROR — A SINGLE HAND, NOTHING ELSE:
At the very edge of the mirror's heavy black frame, one pale adult HAND rests calmly on the
frame — fingers relaxed, the unhurried way someone touches a door handle they have known for
years. The hand simply ends: no arm, no sleeve, no shoulder, no body, no face. It is not
grabbing and not reaching; it is resting. Whoever it belongs to is not in the picture — and
is not looking either. Keep it small, quiet and ambiguous: it should read as "wait… whose
hand is that?", never as a monster. Do NOT render an arm, a figure, or a face anywhere.
```

Und in LIGHTING Punkt 3 ergänzen: *„The hand casts a small hard shadow on the frame — it is
solid, not ghostly."*

**Was B gewinnt:** Serienregel gewahrt (kein klarer Geist), drei Figuren wie B1–B4, ein
Element weniger im Thumbnail, kein Generator-Risiko bei der Pose. Die ruhende Hand knüpft
zudem direkt an Band 4s Geist-Hint an (dort die offene ruhende Hand hinter der Uhr) — ein
stiller Serien-Reim, der die beiden Finale-Bände verbindet.

**Was B verliert:** Die Eskalation „endlich etwas Festes" und das Bild, das das Buch *ist* —
ein Mann, der nicht hinsieht.
