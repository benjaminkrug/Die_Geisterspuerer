## Description (Text zum Einfügen)

```
Spooky. Funny. Impossible to put down.

Something is wrong in the old city of Gravenstedt. For almost two hundred years, its dead haven't been able to leave. They are trapped — and they don't need ghost hunters. They need someone who will listen.

Nora (12) believes in logic, not ghosts. Her little brother Theo (10) believes in everything — especially the things you should run from. Together with Shadow, a mysterious dog with amber eyes who knows when a ghost is near, they stumble into the secret that an old woman upstairs kept for forty years.

Nora and Theo don't fight ghosts. They listen to them.

Every book: a new ghost. Every ghost: a story that wants to be heard. And every story: one more piece of the secret behind Gravenstedt itself.

The Ghost Listeners is a five-book series for brave readers ages 9 to 12. Spooky but safe — chills, not nightmares. After every scare comes something to laugh about.
```
