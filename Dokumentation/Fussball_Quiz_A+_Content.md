# Fertig zum Eintippen in Amazon KDP A+ Content Manager

Stand: 2026-03-24
Zielgruppe: Kinder ab 8 Jahren, Eltern als Käufer

---

## WIE DU A+ CONTENT IN KDP ANLEGST

1. KDP Konto → Buchdetails des Fußball Quiz Buchs
2. Oben: "Inhalt und Beschreibung" → ganz unten: "A+ Inhalt"
3. "Abschnitt hinzufügen" → Modul-Typ auswählen (siehe unten)
4. Text und Bilder einfügen
5. Vorschau prüfen → Einreichen (Amazon braucht 1-7 Tage zur Freigabe)

### BILD 1 — Modul 1 (Banner, volle Breite)
**Zielgröße:** 970 x 300 px (sehr breit, flach — Panorama-Format)
**Verwendung:** Ganz oben im A+ Content, erster Eindruck

**Prompt (direkt kopieren):**
```
Create an ultra-wide panoramic banner illustration (aspect ratio approximately 16:5, very wide and flat) for a German children's football quiz book targeted at kids aged 8 to 12.

COMPOSITION: The image is divided into three visual zones. Left third: a dynamic action scene of a football player in mid-air bicycle kick, foot connecting with the ball, motion blur on the ball showing incredible speed. The player wears a generic white and green kit with no logos or real team branding. Center third: a bird's-eye view fragment of a bright green football pitch with crisp white lines, the center circle visible, under dramatic floodlight beams cutting through a dark evening sky. The stadium in the background is packed with a blurred crowd of thousands of tiny cheering fans in yellow and blue tones. Right third: intentionally cleaner and slightly darker — this area will have text overlaid by Amazon, so keep it visually calm with only a subtle football texture or faint pitch lines.

FLOATING ELEMENTS scattered naturally across the full image: a gleaming gold trophy cup, a large bold number "91" in glowing yellow (referencing Messi's record), a classic black-and-white football, a stopwatch showing a very low number, small golden stars, and a subtle question mark icon. These elements feel like they are floating or flying through the air, giving a sense of motion and excitement.

STYLE: Bold, modern, graphic children's book illustration. Clean vector-art feel with slight painterly texture. NOT photorealistic. Think high-quality Pixar-adjacent illustration — vivid, punchy, full of life.

COLOR PALETTE: Primary colors are rich grass green (#1a8a3c), bright golden yellow (#f5c800), electric blue (#0057b8), and clean white. Dark navy blue (#0a1628) for the sky/background. High contrast. Saturated, not pastel.

LIGHTING: Dramatic stadium floodlights from above-right create strong highlights on the player and the pitch surface. Volumetric light rays visible in the dark sky.

MOOD: Exciting, high-energy, professional yet fun. Makes a child immediately think "I want to know everything about football."

TECHNICAL: No real player faces, no real team logos, no real brand names. No text or letters anywhere in the image. Image must work cropped to a very wide horizontal format without losing the key elements. Seamless horizontal composition.
```
